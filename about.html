Who we are

Engineers and technicians who stay with the work.

Dutam Machineries is a manufacturing team for precision machine parts and Special Purpose Machines. We work across automotive, aerospace, electronics, packaging, pharmaceuticals, and other production industries.

Projects start with your operation and specification. The same group that reviews the brief designs in CAD, machines and fabricates in-house, inspects against the drawing, and supports installation when the work reaches your floor.

The aim is practical: a part or machine that fits the line, runs consistently, and does not leave you guessing after handover.
