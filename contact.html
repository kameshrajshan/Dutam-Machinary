FAQ

Questions before a quote.

What do you manufacture?

Precision machine parts and custom Special Purpose Machines, plus automation, assembly and testing, tooling, CNC components, retrofits, and custom engineering.

Can you build a machine for one operation?

Yes. Special Purpose Machines are designed for a specific production requirement, including automation where the process needs it.

Which materials can you machine?

Mild steel, stainless steel, alloy steel, brass, aluminum, and engineering plastics.

Which industries do you work with?

Automotive, aerospace, electronics, packaging, pharmaceuticals, engineering, food processing, textile, heavy machinery, and consumer goods.

How does a project start?

We analyse the requirement, design it in CAD, manufacture it, inspect it at every stage, then deliver it with installation, training, and support.

Do you support the machine after delivery?

Yes. Delivery includes installation, training, and after-sales support.

Can you improve a machine we already own?

Yes. Retrofit covers control, pneumatic and hydraulic, automation, and mechanical changes.
